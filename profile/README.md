## Our story

It started with a humanoid we bought to test our policies on. Its knee gave out, and we spent two months waiting on a single replacement part while everything ground to a halt. By then it was clear: a robot you can't fix yourself will always hold you back.

So we built our own. First came [a set of legs](https://docs.menlo.ai/asimov/0), then a torso, then [a whole body](https://docs.menlo.ai/asimov/1). Our team couldn't all share one robot, so we also built a simulator and the tools to run it from anywhere. What came out the other side is [Asimov](https://menlo.ai/asimov-1): the robot we wish we'd had, and probably the one you do too.
